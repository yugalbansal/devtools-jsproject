Same three moves, every time.

The tools differ in what they compute. The workflow never changes.

  1. 1.0

    Paste.

    Drop the token, the text, the key in the box. Nothing uploads — there is no upload endpoint to hit, no account to sign into, no spinner warming up.

  2. 2.0

    Compute.

    The browser does the work where it lives: crypto.subtle for hashes, a dedicated Web Worker for diffs, a plain base64 decode for tokens. Your CPU, your tab, no queue.

  3. 3.0

    Copy. Leave.

    Take the result and get back to the thing you were actually doing. The page keeps nothing; closing the tab is the full uninstall.
